Child Safety Standards

Last updated 7 November 2026

SaidHere: The Wall is a place-based service for anonymous, text-only conversations. We prohibit child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE), child sexual abuse material (CSAM), and any behaviour or content that endangers children. These rules apply to all users, Walls, posts and replies.

What is prohibited

Users must not create, solicit, distribute, encourage, link to, or facilitate any of the following:

  • Child sexual abuse material (CSAM), or sexual content involving minors.
  • Grooming, sexual solicitation, coercion, exploitation or abuse of a child.
  • Sextortion, sexual blackmail, trafficking or arrangements for the sexual exploitation of minors.
  • Threats, instructions or communications intended to facilitate child abuse or exploitation.

The app's text-only conversations do not permit images or video uploads, but abusive text, links and solicitations are equally prohibited.

Reporting child safety concerns

Users can open a message's options inside the SaidHere app and select Report. Users can also block an anonymous poster. Reports are not shown to other visitors.

To raise a child safety concern outside an individual post, or to escalate a report, contact orders@saidhere.app with the Wall name, message details and a description of your concern. Please do not send, copy or redistribute suspected illegal material.

Immediate danger: Contact your local emergency services or law enforcement. An in-app report is not a substitute for an emergency report.

Review, moderation and enforcement

SaidHere has limited pre-publication text filtering, in-app reporting and moderation tools. Reported messages may be hidden or removed, and Wall owners and platform administrators can moderate reported posts. We prohibit CSAE whether or not automated filters identify it.

When we become aware of suspected CSAE or CSAM, we will assess the concern and take appropriate protective action, including removing or disabling prohibited content, in accordance with applicable law. Confirmed CSAM will be reported to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) or a relevant regional authority where legally required. Urgent threats may be referred to law enforcement.

Privacy and child safety contact

SaidHere does not show public visitor identities, but retains internal anonymous session identifiers for service operation, safety reporting and abuse prevention. See the Privacy Policy.

The designated contact for child safety questions, concerns and notifications is orders@saidhere.app.
