How ownership works

Buy it. Place it. Let the place speak.

Each physical Wall is linked to its purchaser. On the first scan you name the Wall and set its location, then manage it later through My Walls.

3

On the first scan, name the Wall and set its location. You can change both later in My Walls.

Wall owner controls

You control the Wall, not the people on it.

Posts stay anonymous. Wall owners cannot see who posted a message or identify individual visitors.

What you can control

You manage the place and its moderation — without compromising the anonymity of the people using it.

  • Change the Wall name and location
  • Pause or reactivate the Wall
  • Review reported posts and choose to keep or remove them
