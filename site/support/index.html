Support

Help with SaidHere Walls and NFC plaques.

A plaque does not open the app

On iPhone, hold the top of the phone close to the SaidHere plaque. When the NFC notification appears, tap it. If the app is installed and the plaque is active, the Wall should open directly. If it does not, try the plaque again with the phone unlocked and connected to the internet.

The Wall opens but I cannot write

Writing is only unlocked for a limited time after a valid physical plaque tap. You can continue reading Walls you have already discovered, but you must tap that Wall's plaque again to write or reply after the writing window expires.

A valid plaque says it is inactive

The Wall may have been deactivated or its NFC tag may have been replaced. A replaced plaque URL is intentionally invalidated so copied or damaged tags can be retired without losing the Wall's history.

I found inappropriate content

Use the report control on the message. Reports are anonymous and help hide or remove inappropriate content.

TestFlight feedback

During the beta, use TestFlight's Send Beta Feedback option to report crashes, broken links, NFC problems or other unexpected behaviour. Including a screenshot makes troubleshooting much easier.
