Support

Help with The Wall and its NFC Walls.

A Wall does not open the app

On iPhone, hold the top of the phone close to The Wall. When the NFC notification appears, tap it. If the app is installed and the Wall is active, it should open directly. If it does not, try again with the phone unlocked and connected to the internet.

The Wall opens but I cannot write

Writing is only unlocked for a limited time after a valid physical Wall tap. You can continue reading Walls you have already discovered, but you must tap that Wall again to write or reply after the writing window expires.

A valid Wall says it is inactive

The Wall may have been deactivated or its NFC tag may have been replaced. A replaced NFC URL is intentionally invalidated so copied or damaged tags can be retired without losing the Wall's history.

I found inappropriate content

Use the report control on the message. Reports are anonymous and help hide or remove inappropriate content.

TestFlight feedback

During the beta, use TestFlight's Send Beta Feedback option to report crashes, broken links, NFC problems or other unexpected behaviour. Including a screenshot makes troubleshooting much easier.
